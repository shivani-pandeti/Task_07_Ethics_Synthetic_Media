# Bonus: Internal Announcement of the Policy

This is the internal announcement Ledgerline Analytics would share with staff when adopting the synthetic voice policy. After it, I note what writing it showed me about the policy.

---

## Internal Announcement: New Policy on AI Voices

Ledgerline is adopting a new **Policy on Synthetic Voice and Likeness**. It applies to everyone at the firm and to all client work.

### Why

Clients trust Ledgerline because our numbers are reliable. AI voices save time, but they can make an analysis sound more certain than it is, and short clips can be shared without anyone knowing they are AI-generated. This policy lets us keep using AI voices without risking that trust.

### What is allowed

Using a stock AI voice from an approved tool to narrate Ledgerline's own verified analysis for the client who ordered it.

### What is required

- A second analyst checks every number and caveat before the script is voiced.
- Every narration says it uses an AI voice: at the start, every 60 seconds, and at the end.
- The narration speaks as Ledgerline, never as "I."
- The original file is delivered without editing, so its AI label (C2PA metadata) is not lost.
- Every narration is recorded in the log.

### What is never allowed

- Using anyone's voice without their own written consent
- Using any AI voice for financial results or forecasts shared outside the firm
- Fake interviews, testimonials, or political content

### Your rights

- No one has to lend their voice, and saying no has no consequences.
- Anyone can decline to work on a synthetic-media project.
- Mistakes should be reported to the Responsible Use Lead. Reporting is never punished; hiding a mistake is.

Questions go to the Responsible Use Lead. The full policy is in `phase_b_policy.md`.

## What Writing This Showed Me

The task brief says an announcement can expose parts of a policy that made sense in a draft but are hard to explain out loud. Writing this, I noticed three:

1. **The 60-second reminder will meet pushback.** Explained plainly, it means client narrations get interrupted every minute. Some clients will object, which is the pressure my limitations section warned about.
2. **"No answer means no" could stall projects.** If the Responsible Use Lead is busy, work stops. The policy may need a backup reviewer.
3. **"Saying no has no consequences" is easy to write but hard to make true.** In a small firm, a junior analyst may still feel pressure when a partner asks. Only the partners' behavior can make that promise real.
